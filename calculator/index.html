What happens to X speed?

In ideal projectile motion, gravity does not act along X, so Vx stays constant.

No drag: x(t) = x₀ + Vx₀t   and   Vx(t) = Vx₀

What creates the parabola?

Horizontal position advances while vertical velocity loses about 32.174 ft/s each second. Combining those motions produces the familiar arc.

y(t) = y₀ + Vy₀t − ½gt²

Wind model boundary

Wind is modeled as a simplified aerodynamic coupling that nudges the bag’s horizontal velocity toward the wind velocity. It gives a useful theoretical curve, but it is not a full CFD-grade bag model.

Experimental model. Real bag behavior varies with construction, fill distribution, spin, orientation, flutter, gusts, board finish, humidity, and impact deformation. Use this as a training and exploration tool—not a promise from the universe.
